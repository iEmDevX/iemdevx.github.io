Privacy Policy – English Wan La Nid

Last updated: 24 September 2026

English Wan La Nid is used only by its owner to upload and schedule short English-lesson videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google) data: through the YouTube Data API the app reads the owner's channel ID and title, uploads videos, and deletes videos it uploaded. It does not access any other user's data.
  • Facebook data: only the Page's own access token, used to post Reels to that Page.

How data is stored and shared

  • OAuth tokens are stored privately as encrypted secrets in the owner's own automation environment.
  • No personal information of viewers, followers or other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.
  • The app does not offer sign-in to anyone other than the owner.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
