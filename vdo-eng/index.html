English Wan La Nid

English Wan La Nid (English วันละนิด) is a personal tool that creates short daily English-lesson videos for Thai learners and publishes them to the owner's own channels:

  • YouTube channel: @EnglishWanlanid
  • Facebook Page: English Wan La Nid

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload and schedule Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to remove a scheduled Short when the matching Facebook Reel is removed. It is not offered to other users.
